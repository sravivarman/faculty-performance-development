## Minimum corrective priority

1. **SC-001, SC-004** — unambiguous user-facing wording and missing detail visibility.
2. **SC-002, SC-003, SC-008** — data-entry/reporting clarity without rewriting records or adding new modules.
3. **SC-005 through SC-010** — only after a demonstrated Department reporting need.
